### Apa itu CENGKUDU-OS?

CENGKUDU-OS is an offline-first system built for coffee farmers who work without signal, without paperwork, and without middlemen.

With one voice, a farmer records harvest, fermentation, drying, and sale.
Every batch is traced, verified, and connected directly to buyers.
No internet needed. No complexity. Just the farmer, the land, and the future in his own hands.

CENGKUDU.OS App - Rural Traceability System.
Real-time traceability from seed to sell.
